# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge
We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions kindly.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with care.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome attention.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Report concerns
privately to the maintainers via GitHub (Security tab -> private report, or the contact on the
maintainer's profile). All reports are handled confidentially.
